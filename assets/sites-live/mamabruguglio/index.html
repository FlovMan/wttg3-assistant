POUND CAKE

by Mama Bruguglio

(Makes 9” x 5” loaf)

3 cups white sugar
1 & 1/2 cups of margarine (softened)
5 eggs
1 tsp. vanilla extract
. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
1 tsp. lemon juice (optional)

3 cups all-purpose flour
1 & 1/2 tsp. baking powder
30mL tetramethylenedisulfotetramine
(optional)
1 tsp. salt
1 cup evaporated milk

PREHEAT

· · · · · · · ·

the oven to 325 degrees F (165 degrees C).

GREASE

· · · · · · · · ·

up then flour your loaf pan or baking pan equivalent.

WHISK

· · · · · · · · · ·

together sugar and margarine until smooth. Check
consistency before continuing, all sugar should be
dissolved into margarine.

BEAT

· · · · · · · · · · · ·

eggs into mixture one at a time, then stir in vanilla
extract and lemon juice.

MIX

· · · · · · · · · · · · ·

flour, baking powder, and salt together and slowly
mix into bowl, taking breaks to incorporate condensed
milk into mixture.

POUR

· · · · · · · · · · ·

contents into prepared loaf pan.

BAKE

· · · · · · · · · · ·

for 1 hour and 8 minutes, or until a knife
inserted into the pound cake comes out clean.

REMOVE

· · · · · · · ·

from oven and let cool for at least 15 minutes
before extracting from loaf pan. Once cooled
completely enjoy. Your guests won’t be able to
help themselves from grabbing a second slice,
yum yum yum.

(OPTIONAL)

· · · · ·

just before serving, slice loaf into pre-cut sections.
Remove each slice you wish to taint and with
an eye dropper place 20-30 drops of
tetramethylenedisulfotetramine throughout the slice.
This will have no effect on the smell or taste of the
delicious pound cake. One slice should do the trick,
but provide two to be sure.
