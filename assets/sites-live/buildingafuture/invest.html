Your money matters

We like to keep you abreast to the current happenings during the time your investment is growing. Projects span many weeks before completion and while we are paid throughout the construction process in small sums as progression is made, you will only receive a return when the full project is complete. These updates help create insight as to how your investment is doing and offers a rough estimate of the timeline before your large return is sent.

Waiting until the end allows us to offer the greatest return possible on your initial investment, but also keeps both parties safe from investigation. Siphoning funds constantly is not something we can do. If that is a deal breaker we hope you reconsider and invest at a future time. Putting your trust in us there will result in a net gain, there is no question about it. The only question you have to ask yourself is will you be one of the lucky few to invest before the opportunity has vanished?
