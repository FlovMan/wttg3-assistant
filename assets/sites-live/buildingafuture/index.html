Invest in the future

Invest in a cause that will do more than just thank you for contributing.

We recruit from all around the world

The funds you send us go towards employing team members across the globe to help in constructing some of the largest development projects. We prove how essential your contribution is by multiplying your initial deposit by 2x, 5x, 10x, or more and returning it to you when the job is complete.

This is all made possible by capitalizing on the weak. See a couple bucks is nothing to you or me, but to someone in a war ridden country living in poverty and squalor it's a gift from the gods above. It's easy to sell a hard job to someone who already has a tough life and is desperately clawing for a way out. We promise that way out with no real intention of helping them get there.

Once they're working for us we confiscate their passport, all the money they have, and other personal belongings. They can try and get help, but will have a hard time getting home without money or a passport in a country where they don't even speak the language. Coming to terms with their situation leads to the realization that the easier of the two options is to work for us.
